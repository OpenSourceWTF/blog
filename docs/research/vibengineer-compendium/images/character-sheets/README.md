# Vibengineer Compendium Character Reference

Use these sheets as visual references for future whiteboard illustrations:

- [The Vibengineer](the-vibengineer-model-sheet.png)
- [The Agent](the-agent-model-sheet.png)

## Shared style

- Loose black dry-erase marker and pencil on warm off-white paper
- Sparse red accents for the Vibengineer
- Sparse blue accents for the Agent
- Editorial caricature rather than polished corporate vector art
- Exaggerated but readable expressions
- Post-it notes act as speech, thought, or instruction bubbles

## The Vibengineer

- Slightly messy dark hair and short stubble
- Half-lidded skeptical eyes
- Rolled-sleeve light button shirt, narrow dark tie, dark trousers, sneakers
- Red marker behind the right ear
- Coffee mug with a small red zigzag
- Calm, mildly tired, accountable

## The Agent

- Small rounded box-shaped white head and body
- Large oval eyes and simple expressive mouth
- Thin antenna with a blue ball
- Circular blue ear pieces and one blue chest indicator
- Segmented flexible limbs, rounded hands, simple boots
- Oversized pencil with blue accents
- Eager, fast, helpful, and ready to do the wrong thing confidently

## Continuity rule

The Vibengineer supplies judgment and accountability. The Agent supplies speed and enthusiasm. Neither character is the villain; the joke is the gap between plausible motion and controlled engineering.
