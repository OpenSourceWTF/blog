## Compose the article

1. Open **Articles** in the X side navigation and choose **Write**.
2. Enter the exact title above.
3. Add `vibengineer-01-structure-or-slopware.jpg` as the header image.
4. Paste the body from `article.md`, omitting its leading `#` title.
5. Apply the heading, subheading, bold, quote, numbered-list, and bulleted-list formatting shown in the Markdown.
6. Replace each remaining `[INSERT IMAGE: ...]` marker with the matching file from `posts/images/`.
7. Add the supplied ALT text in X's image-description field and use the supplied caption beneath the image.
8. Remove the marker, ALT, and CAPTION instruction lines after inserting each image. Do not insert the first image twice if it is already the header.
9. Check every link and preview the article on desktop and mobile before publishing.
