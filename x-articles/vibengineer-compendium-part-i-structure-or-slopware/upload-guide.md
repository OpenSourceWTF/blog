## Compose the article

1. Open `preview-for-x.html` in a browser.
2. Use **Copy title**, then paste it into the X Article title field.
3. Add `vibengineer-01-structure-or-slopware.jpg` as the header image.
4. Use **Copy article body**, then paste it into the X Article editor.
5. Replace the remaining black image guides with the matching files from `posts/images/`.
6. Add each yellow ALT line to X's image-description field and retain the supplied caption beneath the image.
7. Remove every black and yellow guide after inserting its image. Do not insert the first image twice if it is already the header.
8. Check every link and preview the article on desktop and mobile before publishing.

If rich copy is blocked, use `article.md` as the fallback and apply the formatting shown there manually.
